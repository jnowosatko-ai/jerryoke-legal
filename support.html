What JerryOke is

JerryOke is a local-first karaoke companion for iPhone. You can keep song lists on your device and find karaoke venues. There is no account or login.

This version does not play, download, or show lyrics in-app. YouTube opens in the YouTube app or Safari. Location is optional and used only to find karaoke venues. If location is off, the map defaults to Colorado Springs.

How to get help

Support is provided through public GitHub Issues:

https://github.com/jnowosatko-ai/jerryoke-legal/issues

Open a new issue and include what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened. Issues are public, so do not include information you do not want posted.
